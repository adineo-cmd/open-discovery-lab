## Track D kickoff — grid Ramsey minima (late night, spilling into 11-10)

### Field survey (5 candidates scored on Novelty/Feasibility/Value)

Winner: **combinatorial enumeration of Ramsey-type grid coloring minima** —
the minimum number of monochromatic axis-parallel rectangles (4 corners
same color) over all 2-colorings of a grid. OEIS A399620 (n×n) and
A399621 (k×n triangle) were authored by Jeremy F. Alm on 2026-09-05 with
values only through n=8 and the `more` keyword — the OEIS itself asks for
the next terms. Prior-art check 2026-11-09: no paper/preprint computes
a(9) or row 9 (closest: 2015 MIP for monochromatic *squares*; Fenner–
Gasarch rectangle-free program; arXiv:0810.3019 (c,t)-guaranteed grids;
Dumitru arXiv:2512.23303 Dec 2025); GitHub code search finds no public
minimization solver. Scored and rejected: peaceable queens a(16) (exact
values stop at n=15, Tabatabai 2018; ILP bounds only for 16+ — proof cost
out of budget), number-theory records (mined by distributed projects),
polyomino/SAW enumeration (frontiers cost supercomputer-days), Serratus-
style SRA mining (bandwidth infeasible here).

### Method developed (the lab's contribution)

Two-stage exact proof composition:
1. **Aggregation lower bound** (`tools/relax_lb.py`): mono =
   Σ_pairs [C(a00,2)+C(a11,2)], Σ a00 = Σ_c C(z_c,2) etc. — solve the
   relaxation exactly with CP-SAT (~0.1 s). Bound is *exactly tight* at
   n=6,7,8 (gives 6, 21, 40 = the OEIS values) and gives LB(9,9)=74,
   LB(10,10)=130.
2. **Construction search** (`tools/min_mono_rect.py`, count model): CP-SAT
   with per-row-pair count variables, x[0][0]=0 + row-lex symmetry
   breaking, LB added as constraint; every witness re-counted by an
   independent pure-Python counter.
