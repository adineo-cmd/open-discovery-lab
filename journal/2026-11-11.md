### Container format (F-18)

**Zero ELFs in the firmware partition set.** MTK wraps everything in the
mtk_hdr record chain (magic 0x58881688, 512-B headers, ext record
0x58891689 @0x30) with per-payload dual DER X.509 signing (1709 B + 957 B);
modem data payloads unsigned; md1dsp GFH record type 260 with `DSP!` magic
(beyond U-Boot's enum). First vendor-image byte-for-byte confirmation of
zrax's synthetic-fixture socboot2 model.

### Registry correction

Lab census.py e_machine table had errors (195:"TILE64", 93:"TI PRU").
Corrected: binutils owns 195=ARCv2 (EM_ARC_COMPACT2); TILE64=187,
TILEGx=191, TI_PRU=144. Fixed in mediatek/tools/census_mtk.py and the
umbrella tools/census.py.
