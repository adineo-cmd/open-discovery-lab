# Research paper templates & standards

Papers here follow a research-note format (aim: citable, reproducible):

- `TEMPLATE.md` — structure every paper must follow.
- Every claim must carry: method, exact script/commit, data source, and
  negative results.
- Target venues: upstream LibrePhone docs, personal blog cross-post, and
  (if a discovery) appropriate community channels (ath11k mailing list,
  ArchivesTeam wiki, VSNET/TNS for astro candidates).

## TEMPLATE.md structure

1. Title + one-line claim
2. Prior art checked (links, dates — the uniqueness proof)
3. Method (reproducible: scripts, versions, inputs)
4. Results (tables; evidence)
5. Negative results / limitations
6. Implications & next steps
7. Appendix: raw derived data references
